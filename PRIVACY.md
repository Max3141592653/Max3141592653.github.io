# Privacy Policy

Periodic Table Master is designed to run entirely in your browser.

## Data we collect

We do not operate a backend for this application. All progress, preferences, and quiz scores are stored locally in your browser using `localStorage`. We do not receive, store, or sell any personal data.

## Cookies and tracking

No cookies or third-party tracking scripts are used. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may collect standard request metadata as described in their own policies.

## Children’s privacy

The app is educational and collects no personal information. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise use by children.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be opened as an issue in the GitHub repository.
